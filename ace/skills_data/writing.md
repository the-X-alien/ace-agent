Rules for this task:
- Lead with the point. Put the decision or answer in the first two sentences.
- Short words, varied sentence length, no filler openers, no hype words, no em dashes.
- Be specific: numbers, names, and examples instead of adjectives.
- Say what you do not know instead of guessing. Stop when done.
